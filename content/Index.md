## Welcome! 🌱

This website is for publishing my part-time research project while it happens. During the research i build a body of texts in a format of [hypertext](https://www.w3.org/WhatIs.html), with a simple markup language (fin. _merkintäkieli_) called [Markdown](https://www.markdownguide.org/getting-started/). What that means, is that these texts link into each other constituting _a non-linear repository where the contents relate to each other_. For a taste of this you can jump straight into the [[Actor-Network Theory]] note, my [[Research Plan]] or see examples of similar (or way fancier) implementations in the [[Digital Garden]] note. You can also see the graphic interpretation on the right sidebar, where you can also change between local and global view.

All of this is managed in a personal knowledge base system called [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/). This repository in Obsidian is called a Vault. When it's published, it can be described with terms such as [Digital Garden](https://maggieappleton.com/garden-history/) or [Wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki) (without the possibility for reader editing). This site has been translated straight from my Obsidian Vault into a www-publishable HTML with [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/). It is then hosted in and published with [GitHub](https://github.com/about) and it's Pages functionality.

> [!info]
> This is very much **a work in progress**. The maturity of the content lives and that's kinda the whole point of it. Not a single note is permanent, fixed or "ready". Also not everything in my Vault will be published right away. This site itself is _a static epoch of my Vault_ and has to be manually updated into the web whenever wanted to.
