A digital garden is a metaphor for an online space where thoughts, ideas, and notes are cultivated and interconnected over time. In online space it's distinct e.g. from a traditional blog in several key ways. Unlike many types of texts, which are typically published and then left as static pieces, digital garden entries are meant to be living documents that are constantly updated, revised, and expanded upon. The focus is on the process of developing ideas, rather than presenting finished products.

Digital gardens often utilize extensive internal linking to create a network of interconnected ideas. This somewhat resembles the idea of [[Pattern Language]] and allows readers to explore related concepts and follow the evolution of the author's thinking. This linking creates a non linear way of navigating the information. Digital gardens are often highly personal spaces where individuals can explore their own interests and develop their own unique perspectives. It is a place for the author to cultivate their own thoughts.

In essence, a digital garden is a space for continuous learning, reflection, and connection, where ideas are nurtured and allowed to grow organically.
