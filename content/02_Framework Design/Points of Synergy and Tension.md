| Dimension                    | Actor-Network Theory (ANT)                                                                                                 | National Systems of Innovation (NIS)                                                                                                                 | Synergy/Tension in Combination                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level of Analysis**        | "Micro-level"; focuses on specific interactions and relations between individual actors                                    | Macro/meso-level; focuses on systemic structures, institutions, and aggregated outcomes                                                              | **Synergy:** ANT provides granular detail for NIS macro-level observations, bridging the micro-macro                                    |
| **Focus**                    | Heterogeneous networks, agency of human and non-human actors, processes of translation and controversy                     | Institutional interactions, collective learning, knowledge diffusion, policy frameworks, economic performance                                        | **Synergy:** ANT explains how NIS processes are enacted through specific network dynamics                                               |
| **Key Concepts**             | Actor, Network, Translation, Generalized Symmetry, Black Box, Heterogeneity                                                | Institutions, actors (firms, universities, government), interactive learning, knowledge diffusion                                                    | **Synergy:** Concepts like "translation" can operationalize "interactive learning"; "non-human actants" expand the scope of "actors"    |
| **Agency**                   | Distributed across human and non-human actants; agency is an effect of network relations                                   | Primarily focuses on human actors and institutional capacities                                                                                       | **Synergy:** ANT's radical symmetry highlights the active role of technologies, environment, and infrastructure in innovation systems   |
| **View of Innovation**       | Emergent outcome of complex, dynamic, and often contested network formations and stabilizations                            | Result of systemic interactions, institutional support and knowledge flows within a defined system                                                   | **Synergy:** ANT offers a processual, constructivist view to NIS's systemic, outcome-oriented perspective                               |
| **Strengths of Combination** | Provides "mechanistic" explanations for NIS phenomena; reveals "true actors"; offers a dynamic view of system evolution    | Provides a systemic context and questions for ANT's "micro"-analyses; offers policy relevance and a framework for understanding systemic performance | **Overall Synergy:** Creates a more comprehensive, dynamic, and critically informed understanding of innovation processes               |
| **Potential Tensions**       | Perceived lack of political critique; difficulty in operationalizing; denies social structures; can be seen as too "local" | May fall into structuralist interpretations; may overlook power dynamics and micro-level complexities; can be seen as too "global"                   | **Tension:** Reconciling ANT's agnosticism and focus on local processes with NIS's normative/policy-oriented goals and structural focus |
