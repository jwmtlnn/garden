Immutable mobile is a concept referring to an entity or object that is both stable and transportable, meaning it holds its shape or identity while moving across time and space. It appears as a stable, fixed, and unified entity because of its [[Punctualisation]], allowing others to disregard its underlying complexities and assumptions. [[@latourLaboratoryLifeSocial1986]]. A good example presented by [[@walshamActorNetworkTheoryResearch1997]]p.469-470) of an immutable mobile, is the idea of "software as frozen discource"; an [[Inscription]] which resists change and displays properties of irreversibility. On the contrary the well known study of the Zimbabwe Bush Pump by [[@delaetZimbabweBushPump2000]] exhibits a mutable mobile, where the pump undergoes transformations and adaptations while traveling.
