The general description of heterogeneity signals something consisting of dissimilar, diverse or different elements [[@merriam-websterHeterogeneity]]. In actor-network theory the concept emphasizes the study of how diverse elements, both human and nonhuman, are interconnected and contribute to the formation of [[Actor-Network|networks]], referring to the recognition of the world as composed of a [[Multiplicity]] of different kinds of connections, entities and realities [[@latourReassemblingSocialIntroduction2005]];[[@lawMethodMessSocial2004]]. In understanding this notion the principle of [[Generalized Symmetry]] is crucial.

ANT posits that [[Actor|actors]] and their networks are fundamentally heterogeneous, composed of a mix of human and nonhuman elements without a specific homogeneous morphism or other purity-seeking classification as a rule [[@latourActornetworkTheoryFew1996]]p.370;380).
