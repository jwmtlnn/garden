Activity Theory (AT) is a theoretical framework that approaches the study of technical innovations by aiming to transcend the dualism between subject and object, and between nature and society. It posits that human activity is mediated by [[Artefact|artefacts]], which can include tools, signs, and representations. These artifacts play a crucial role in structuring activity and influencing human behavior. Activity Theory is sometimes compared with [[Actor-Network Theory]] due to their shared attempt to transcend subject-object dualisms. While ANT may emphasize [[Generalized Symmetry]] between human and non-human entities, AT focuses on the mediated structure of human activity and its socio-cultural context.
